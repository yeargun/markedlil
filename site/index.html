Official unminified ESM, then the same file through Oxc and Terser with mangling enabled and disabled. @itslil/marked is the LilScript compiler output. It is not post-minified.

Lane Raw gzip-9 Brotli-11 vs Oxc mangle

Why the official minify lanes exist. Comparing LilScript to uncompressed marked.esm.js is not fair. Oxc and Terser, with and without mangling, are the same work the JS ecosystem actually ships. The published LilScript artifact is still the compiler-selected module.
